# Design

## Scene

A crew member reads a wall instrument in a dark cabin. The page is the cabin: pure near-black, no tint. One cobalt lamp marks selection and “nominal.” Brass marks “watch.” A red mark means act. Ivory type does the reading.

## Color

- bg `oklch(0.10 0 0)`
- surface `oklch(0.15 0 0)`
- ink `oklch(0.96 0.008 230)`
- muted `oklch(0.78 0.012 230)`
- primary `oklch(0.62 0.11 232)` with white text on fills
- brass `oklch(0.82 0.11 82)`
- alarm `oklch(0.72 0.15 27)`

Strategy: restrained. Most of the screen is black and type. Color is status.

## Type

IBM Plex Sans for the interface. IBM Plex Mono for numbers only. Both are bundled, so the instrument still reads with the network down.

## Layout

The nameplate is once. The day scrubber and four story marks are the time control. The twin is the object. The finding is the action. Rings are one row of instruments, not cards. The chart is one series.
