# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A crew member on a long mission, using one screen to see whether today’s change is the cabin or themselves, and what to do before Earth can answer. Judges of a space-health demo are the second audience. The Briefing section is for them.

## Purpose

Show personal-baseline drift across the five NASA human-spaceflight hazards, attribute the cause, and offer a sourced next step. The twin makes the slow cost of dropping countermeasures visible.

## Personality

A ship’s instrument at night. Quiet, specific, unwilling to perform medicine it does not have.

## Anti-references

Neon cockpit HUDs, population-average dashboards, chatbot doctors, cream-and-card marketing pages.

## Accessibility

Text labels sit beside every color. The body map has a button list. Focus is visible. Motion is limited to state changes.
